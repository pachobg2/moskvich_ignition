Factory maps are built from the distributor data tables: static timing + centrifugal advance (rpm) + vacuum advance (MAP, faded in above idle). You can edit a factory slot; “Regenerate” restores its factory curve.
Tap a cell to select it. Turn Range on (or hold Shift on a PC) to tap a second corner. Changes go to the ECU instantly; they are saved to flash when you press Save or the engine stops.

LPG ignition timing

LPG burns slower than petrol, so it usually wants more advance. The timing switches when the valve signal is present.
° (+ = more advance)
Added to every cell of the petrol map while LPG is active (still limited by Max advance and the trigger angle). Changes are eased in, not stepped.

Dwell vs battery voltage

How long the coil charges before each spark. Measure your coil: current should reach ~3.5–4 A (scope on a shunt) without the IGBT getting hot. Voltage is interpolated between rows.

PointSensor mVkPa 1 (low) 2 (high)
Works with any linear 1-bar analog sensor. Typical: point 2 = engine off at atmospheric pressure (type the local kPa, then Capture); point 1 = a known vacuum from a hand pump (e.g. 40 kPa absolute = 60 kPa below atmosphere) — or the datasheet values. Defaults are an MPX4115.
kPa
Enter the current local pressure (≈101.3 at sea level; ≈ 95 at 500 m). Adjusts the offset only.

Timing light

Locks the spark at a fixed advance (ignores the map, idle control and rev retard). Use at idle to confirm the real timing matches what the ECU thinks, then adjust Trigger angle in Setup until they agree. Turns itself off when the ECU restarts.
° BTDC

Bench simulator

Generates trigger events internally so you can check spark timing on the bench with a scope or timing light — no engine needed. Remove the coil from the engine harness first; disable before connecting to a running engine.
